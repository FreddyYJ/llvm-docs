TemplateArgument* clang::RedeclarableTemplateDecl::CommonBase::InjectedArgs = nullptr

The set of "injected" template arguments used within this template.

This pointer refers to the template arguments (there are as many template arguments as template parameaters) for the template, and is allocated lazily, since most templates do not require the use of this information.
